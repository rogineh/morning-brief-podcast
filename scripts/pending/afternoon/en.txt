Advocacy groups say the data reinforces long-standing calls for faster processing of home care applications, arguing that for many older Australians, access to even modest in-home support can make the difference between staying safely at home and needing far more intensive, costly care. The federal government says it's continuing to work through the existing backlog and has pointed to recent funding increases as evidence it's taking the issue seriously, though advocates say the pace of improvement still isn't matching the scale of demand.

Residents in coastal and regional areas, including Livingstone Shire, are being urged to check their emergency kits, trim overhanging trees, and make sure they know their local evacuation routes well ahead of any severe weather, rather than scrambling once a warning is issued.
