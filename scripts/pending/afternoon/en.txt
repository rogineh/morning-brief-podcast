Others say the scale of the employment decline, combined with recent signs of slowing wage growth, now paints a clearer picture of an economy losing momentum more broadly, strengthening the case for a more supportive approach to monetary policy heading into the new year. Mortgage holders and borrowers more broadly will be watching closely for any further signals from central bank officials in the coming weeks, with commentary from board members likely to be parsed intensely for clues about which way the debate is leaning internally.

In Queensland, early site works are continuing on the new Rockhampton TAFE Excellence Precinct, a sixty-one-million-dollar project that will consolidate vocational training and higher education facilities onto a single campus in the regional city. The precinct is being built on land already home to CQUniversity's Rockhampton campus, allowing the university and TAFE Queensland to share infrastructure and create a more integrated pathway for students moving between vocational and university-level study. Authorities say the facility is expected to be fully operational by the middle of 2028, with construction continuing to ramp up over the coming months as the project moves beyond its initial site preparation phase. The investment forms part of a broader state government push to expand training infrastructure across regional Queensland specifically, aimed at addressing skills shortages in industries like construction and advanced manufacturing that have struggled to find enough qualified workers outside the state's major population centres. Local industry groups have welcomed the project, saying improved access to quality vocational training closer to home will help keep more young people and skilled workers in regional communities rather than having them relocate to Brisbane or elsewhere to access equivalent facilities.

It'll mark a return of major international cricket to the Gold Coast venue, giving local fans a rare chance to watch the national team compete without travelling to one of the larger capital city grounds that typically host the bulk of international fixtures. Cricket administrators say spreading matches across a broader range of venues, including regional and secondary market grounds like the Gold Coast, helps grow the sport's footprint and gives fans in those areas more direct access to top-level international cricket. With tickets on sale and strong local interest already building, organisers are expecting a lively atmosphere for what's shaping up as a significant night on the city's sporting calendar.
