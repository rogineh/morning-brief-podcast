Canada and Germany have jointly committed up to three hundred million dollars to LawZero, the Montreal-based non-profit founded by AI pioneer Yoshua Bengio, to help fund development of what he calls Scientist AI, a system trained on a base of verifiable facts rather than being designed to pursue its own goals or act autonomously. The organisation, which has grown to nearly fifty staff since launching last year, says the funding will go toward research and buying access to a new dedicated computing cluster, with the broader aim of building an alternative to today's frontier models that Bengio argues can exhibit troubling traits like sycophancy and manipulation, reasoning transparently instead and producing evidence-based outputs unclouded by any goals of its own.
