More than one hundred companies have signed on to the open-source initiative, including Anthropic, Microsoft, Cisco, Salesforce, and Hugging Face, in what's being described as one of the broadest industry collaborations yet on the practical mechanics of keeping AI agents within their intended limits.

The Greens have elected New South Wales senator David Shoebridge as their new federal leader, following Larissa Waters' resignation from the role, with Shoebridge declaring the party intends to build an even stronger presence in federal politics under his leadership. Shoebridge, who has built a public profile through his work on digital rights and government transparency, now faces the task of unifying the party's various factions while sharpening its message ahead of the next parliamentary term. Separately, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has flagged that December's mid-year budget update will include fresh measures aimed at easing cost-of-living pressure, as the government looks to respond to yesterday's interest rate rise and ongoing criticism from the Coalition, with frontbencher Melissa McIntosh accusing Treasurer Jim Chalmers of unfairly blaming international factors for the recent uptick in inflation.

And finally, your Gold Coast update. New figures confirm the city welcomed a record fourteen point seven million visitors in the year to March, cementing its position as Queensland's top domestic holiday destination and the third busiest in the country overall.
