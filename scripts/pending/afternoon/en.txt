The government says the laws respond directly to community frustration over repeat youth offending and are needed to keep serious offenders off the streets, while legal advocates and some Indigenous community groups have criticised the approach as politically driven rather than evidence-based, warning it risks further straining an already stretched youth detention system. The bill is still before parliament, and the government has indicated it wants the new offence in place before the end of the year, setting up further debate over the coming weeks as the legislation moves through its remaining stages.
