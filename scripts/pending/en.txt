And finally, your Gold Coast update. Mary the Tasmanian devil has been released back into her public enclosure at Paradise Country wildlife park today, nearly three months after an escape that saw her clear a metre-and-a-half fence with what keepers called an abnormally large leap and disappear into nearby bushland for weeks. The two-year-old spent time recovering in a specialist wildlife hospital after being found suffering brain inflammation, and her habitat has since been fitted with new features, including domed ledges, specifically designed to stop her or any other resident from getting the toe-hold needed to make another escape attempt, with staff at the park saying they're relieved to have her back on display after what became one of the more closely followed animal welfare stories of the year on the Gold Coast.
