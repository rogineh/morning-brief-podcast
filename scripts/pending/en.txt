Advocacy groups say the findings point to a serious gap between diagnosis and actual day-to-day support, particularly in regional areas where specialist services can be thin on the ground, and are calling for better-funded referral pathways so families aren't left piecing together care arrangements without guidance in the weeks after a diagnosis. The survey's authors say the findings are especially significant given Australia's ageing population, with government projections released only last week forecasting the number of Australians living with dementia to rise substantially over coming decades as the population grows older.
