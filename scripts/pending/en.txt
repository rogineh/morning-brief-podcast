Elsewhere, a Senate committee has recommended passing a bill that would strip away the higher private health insurance rebate currently available to Australians aged sixty-five and over, moving everyone onto the same flat rebate rate regardless of age. More than three million older Australians hold private health cover, and the Coalition has vowed to block the bill in the Senate, while the Greens say they won't support it in its current form, reportedly pushing for an exemption for pensioners. The bill's fate, and the government's planned start date for the change, now rests on how those negotiations play out in the weeks ahead. Under the current system, policyholders aged sixty-five to sixty-nine receive a slightly higher rebate than younger Australians, with those over seventy receiving an even larger one, a structure the government argues no longer makes sense given rising life expectancy and workforce participation among older Australians.

The operation, run under the banner of Taskforce Morpheus, resulted in twenty arrests and sixty-nine charges, with authorities describing the gang as a high threat group linked to drug trafficking, money laundering, and violence. Officers also carried out firearms and bail compliance checks as part of the broader operation, which authorities say is aimed at disrupting the gang's ability to operate across state and even international lines. Police say operations like this one rely heavily on intelligence sharing between agencies, and that sustained, coordinated pressure over time has proven more effective at disrupting organised crime networks than isolated, one-off raids.

In better news for local councils, the federal government has backed away from its proposed changes to natural disaster recovery funding arrangements, following sustained pushback from Queensland mayors who'd warned the changes could leave some councils facing financial ruin. The Local Government Association of Queensland and regional council groups have welcomed the reversal, saying it would otherwise have taken years to repair roads, bridges, and other damaged infrastructure after major weather events under the proposed funding model. Council groups say they'll now be pushing for a formal commitment that similar changes aren't revisited without proper consultation with local government in future.
