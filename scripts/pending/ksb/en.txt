Local residents have been sharing photos of hazy skies across the hinterland suburbs, with some describing visibility as noticeably reduced during the thickest periods of smoke, though authorities say conditions are expected to clear once the current round of burns is completed in the coming days. Fire crews say the hinterland's dense bushland makes it a priority area for hazard reduction each year, and thank residents for their patience while the necessary work is carried out ahead of what's shaping up as another demanding bushfire season.
