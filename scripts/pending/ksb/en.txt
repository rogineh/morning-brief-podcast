One beekeeper described losing close to five hundred thousand bees in total, with the entire breeding system of each affected hive, queen included, taken in the raids, a blow that comes on top of the added stress of a destructive mite recently detected in the same region.

And finally, your Gold Coast update. City council has unveiled a twenty-year vision calling for a hundred and eighty-five thousand new homes to be built across the city, almost double the current rate of approvals, as it looks to keep pace with population growth that has helped the local economy expand by twenty-five percent since 2020 to more than fifty-five billion dollars, making it Australia's largest economy outside of a capital city and reinforcing its case as one of the fastest-growing regions in the country. The plan has already sparked concern among some residents worried about further changes to the city's character and lifestyle after decades of rapid growth, with council spending eighteen months shaping the long-term vision before releasing it publicly. On a lighter note, the Gold Coast Turf Club hosts its Pink Ribbon Raceday tomorrow, with a full card of thoroughbred racing running alongside fundraising efforts for the National Breast Cancer Foundation, drawing crowds who come as much for the cause as for the races themselves.
