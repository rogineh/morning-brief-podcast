In Queensland, a brand-new cardiac hybrid theatre has officially opened at Rockhampton Hospital, giving Central Queenslanders access to a range of specialised heart procedures that previously required travel to Brisbane or other major centres. The twenty-eight-and-a-half-million-dollar facility combines a fully equipped interventional cardiology procedure room with advanced imaging technology, alongside a dedicated echocardiogram room and six new recovery bays, taking the hospital's overall cardiology ward capacity up to twelve beds. Doctors at the facility will now be able to perform procedures including coronary angiograms, coronary stent placements, and pacemaker implantation locally, significantly reducing the need for cardiac patients in the region to be transferred hundreds of kilometres away for specialist treatment. Hospital staff say the new theatre represents one of the most significant upgrades to cardiac care in Central Queensland in years, with the facility already welcoming its first patients following completion of construction. Health authorities say better access to cardiac care closer to home is particularly important for a region with a large rural and remote population, where distance to specialist treatment has historically been one of the biggest barriers facing patients experiencing serious heart conditions.
