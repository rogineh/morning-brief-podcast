Now, here's what's happening on the Gold Coast.

Construction firm Multiplex has been formally appointed to deliver the new Coomera Hospital, a major project that will eventually grow to six hundred beds once fully built out. The facility will be delivered in stages, with the first components beginning construction now and the full six hundred beds targeted within the next several years. An independent review of the project's earlier planning found the original scope had been significantly underfunded, prompting the government to revise and expand the plan before construction began. Local residents in the fast-growing northern Gold Coast corridor have welcomed the news, saying a major new hospital is sorely needed to keep pace with the area's rapid population growth, with several existing facilities in the region already stretched well beyond their original capacity during peak periods.

Meanwhile, plans to extend the Gold Coast's light rail network south from Burleigh Heads to Coolangatta have been officially shelved, after a review found a clear majority of residents in the city's southern suburbs opposed the project. In its place, the council and state government say they'll instead focus on improved bus services and smaller infrastructure upgrades in the area, alongside a broader study into transport links across the southern Gold Coast and out to the airport. Some transport advocates have expressed disappointment at the decision, while many local residents have welcomed it, saying the original plan would have caused too much disruption to established neighbourhoods.
